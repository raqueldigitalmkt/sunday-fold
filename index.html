Sunday Fold · Brighton

A little Sunday,
every day.

Your everyday pause, made a little better.

Sunday Fold began with Sunday get-togethers for friends. Lucy Bennett baked the bread and pastries, and Daniel Reed made the coffee.

Today, Lucy leads the baking while Daniel looks after the coffee and welcomes customers. Together, they bring that same feeling of being welcomed into a friend’s home to their neighbourhood bakery in Brighton.
